## My CSC340 Goals
My goals for this course is to build a strong practical understanding of how software systems are planned, designed, and developed. I plan to learn and apply object-oriented modeling techniques such as UML, on real projects using them from the requirements stage through implementation and testing.
I also hope to grow as a teammate by contributing effectively in groups and following professional and ethical standards. In addition, I want to get better at evaluating existing software systems and explaining technical ideas clearly in both written and in class presentations.
By the end of this course, my goal is to have a solid foundation in software engineering that prepares me to handle real-world problems and deliver working solutions on time and within reasonable constraints.
